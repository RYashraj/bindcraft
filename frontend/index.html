BindCraft Help

What is BindCraft?

BindCraft helps B.Pharm students learn AMBER molecular dynamics (MD) without writing command files. You upload prepared structures, choose simple settings, and BindCraft generates the AMBER input files for you.

Three Modes

  • Demo — explore a pre-built AChE + Donepezil simulation. No software required.
  • Generate Only — create and download a complete AMBER input package. Works on Windows without WSL or AmberTools.
  • Run Local — execute the full pipeline on your machine. Requires WSL2 + Ubuntu + AmberTools (free).

Important: No Docking

BindCraft does not perform docking. Your ligand must already be in its binding pose (as a HETATM record in the PDB). Use a co-crystal structure from PDB or perform docking separately with AutoDock Vina or similar tools before uploading.

Quick Start

  1. Click New Project on the dashboard.
  2. Upload your complex PDB + ligand SDF/MOL2 + enter net charge.
  3. Complete the Setup Wizard.
  4. Review warnings and confirm.
  5. Choose Generate or Run.
